# 2026-08-26 — The Girl Who Was Never Called Back

Room 12 of the old **dorm** had a bad reputation. Students moved in every autumn, and every autumn one of them left at three in the morning, **searching for** somewhere else to sleep.

Mara was **suspicious (of)** ghost stories. Her plan was simple: live there one month and **refute** the whole legend.

The dorm stood on an old farm — in the yard there was still a stone trough where a **draft animal** once drank. A century ago a girl **named Nelly** had slept in the **nursery** upstairs. One winter night she climbed onto the **roof** to watch the snow, and her mother forgot to **call her back inside**. By morning she was dead. The family decided to **cremate** her **instead of** burying her, and kept the ashes in the house. **Since** the ashes never left, some **claim**, neither did Nelly.

**Meanwhile**, small things began. Something dragged a **claw** across Mara's door. The mirror above the **bathtub** fogged over and four words appeared, **written in blood**: *I am here.* In her notebook Mara wrote that the girl was **there** — reporting it, not believing it. But her **suspicion** was turning into something colder, and her own senses had started to **betray** her.

Her **fiancée** Ana, the only **survivor** of a house fire years before, came to help. "You two **have something in common**," Ana said quietly. "Nobody came back for either of you."

They began to **track down** the truth. A priest had **performed exorcism** in this room in 1912 and failed; **over time** his diary was forgotten. It said the ghost **can't stand her own reflection** — mirrors were **a lure**, not a weapon. The ashes **bound** her to the walls, and something far older was **at work** here.

Mara's hands began to **shake**. She **accidentally** knocked the mirror down and cut her palm — a small **injury** — and **dozens** of flies rose from the empty frame in a black **swarm**.

At dawn they buried the ashes under the trough, and Nelly was **released**. Then they **got on** the first bus out.
