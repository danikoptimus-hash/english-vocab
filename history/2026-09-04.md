## 3. The House on the Deserted Road

## 4. The Thief Who Wore Faces

It liked **to frame innocent people**. It **approved of** nothing so much as watching somebody else **arrested for** what it had done. In the notebook they found afterwards, one line came back on every page: *all it can do is steal*.

The fight was short. Cruz **threw** a chair through the window to wake the neighbours, and the thing was **released** from its shape, unable to hold **those** borrowed features any longer.

The teacher **regained** her own name a week later, after a magistrate **sent** the case **out of** court. **Accidentally**, the department destroyed the only **film** of the arrest — the one recording that might have proved what Cruz saw.

## 5. Ten Days in the Forest

Nobody expects a **plane crash** on a short flight.

Twenty minutes after **takeoff** the cabin filled with **smoke** so **thick** that Ana could not see the seat in front of her. She had to **tear** the belt loose with both hands and **tear away** a panel to **get out of** the fuselage. The wing came down in a lake; three passengers **drowned** before anyone could **rush to help** them. Ana pulled two of them **out of the water** herself. One man was already at the **bottom**, where nobody could reach him.

**Almost all of them** had some **injury**. Ana counted eleven **survivors** on the shore. The official **searching for** the wreck had begun on the third day — in the wrong valley.

They cut a length of nylon **cord** from a cargo net and used it for everything: a washing line, a splint, a handle for the water can. That single **cord** was the most useful object they owned.

They built a **tent** out of seat covers. One man knew something about **hunting** and set a snare; on the fourth day they ate **a deer** and slept under its **fur**. An old farmer came round the lake with a **draft animal** pulling a cart, and for two days that horse was the only rescue **at work** in the whole valley.

The insects were the worst of it. A **swarm** rose off the water every evening, **dozens** of black **beetles** crawling over the food. They learned to **fill up** every container before dusk and **cut off** the lamp early.

**Until** the tenth morning nobody came. Then, as **the sun came up**, a **ray** caught the tail section and a helicopter finally saw them.

Ana was **completely different** afterwards. She would never **get on** a plane again. The first thing she did at home, in her quiet **residential area**, was sit in the **bathtub** for an hour with the door open, staring at a tube of **toothpaste** as though she had never seen one before.

A journalist wrote that trauma **transforms** people **into** better versions of themselves. Ana **refuted** that in a single line: *it gives nothing back; **all it can do is steal**.*

There was **a tense fight** with her sister about the interviews. After that, Ana stopped **dealing with** the press at all.
